Who we are

Something greater
than ourselves.

Deerfields · Horse Shoe, North Carolina

BREATHE is a community grounded in the belief that everyone has a higher purpose. Alongside Mother Nature we manifest an environment for each individual to discover that purpose, write their own story, and achieve their highest potential.

The workshops, classes, performances, discussions, walks, and games that fill the weekend aren't created by a hired programming team. They're created by people in this community who are passionate about something and feel called to share it.

Over the years that has meant medicinal plant walks, movement practices, storytelling circles, slackline workshops, music experiences, philosophy discussions, creative arts, breathwork, and countless things we never could have imagined on our own.

We dissolve the barriers between the seen and the unseen, revealing a community that is whole, inter-connected, and free.

What holds us together

The Jelly

Anchored by the balancing of mind, body, and human spirit

i. Mind

Share what you love

Through the selfless service of sharing our passions as teachers we build confidence, initiative, and connection. As students we gain new perspectives and sparks to take with us.

ii. Body

Do what you thought you couldn't

Through physical practices we challenge our own belief system of what our bodies are capable of — priming us to more fully receive and integrate the weekend.

iii. Spirit

Give without expectation

Through giving and receiving without expectation we understand all is connected. The human spirit is the Jelly that forms a lasting, meaningful connection between all of us.

Plan your visit

Deerfields.
Horse Shoe, NC.

Twenty minutes south of Asheville, and twenty from the airport. Primitive camping is included with a weekend pass, in Elysian Fields and Valhalla. Free overnight parking is onsite, within walking distance of both campgrounds.

Potable well water is free; ice is available to purchase. There are no indoor toilets or showers — we rely on port-o-johns and outdoor showers. Come prepared to be self-reliant, and lean on a neighbor to fill the gaps.

There are no food vendors onsite. A Community Meal Prep Tent has shared stoves, tables, chairs, and basic utensils.
