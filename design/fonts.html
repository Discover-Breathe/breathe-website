The vibe I inferred from your copy. The writing is warm, plain-spoken and a bit earthy — "the Jelly", "dissolve the barriers between the seen and the unseen", "no vendors, no spectacle, just a community building the thing it wants to attend", "lean on a neighbor". Primitive camping, well water, drumming, breathwork, leave-no-trace. That points to:
  • Humanist, not geometric — made by people, not a design system
  • Warm and slightly irregular — nothing corporate or techy
  • Confident but unhurried — no luxury-fashion thinness, no hype-bold shouting
  • Plainly legible — an all-ages community, not a design conference
