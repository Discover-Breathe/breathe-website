An introduction

Something greater
than ourselves.

Through shared learning, physical practice, and a spirit of genuine connection, BREATHE brings mind, body, and spirit into alignment, uniting individuals into something greater than themselves.

The workshops, classes, performances, discussions, walks, and games that fill the weekend aren't created by a hired programming team. They're created by people in this community who are passionate about something and feel called to share it. Over the years that has meant medicinal plant walks, movement practices, storytelling circles, slackline workshops, philosophy discussions, breathwork, and countless things we never could have imagined on our own.

We gather at Deerfields — a beautiful natural setting that invites us to slow down, connect, and live simply together for the weekend. No vendors. No spectacle. Just a community building the thing it wants to attend.

What holds us together

The Jelly

We are anchored by the balancing of mind, body, and human spirit — three things that only make sense together.

i.

Mind

Through the selfless service of sharing our passions as teachers we build confidence, initiative, and connection. As students we gain new perspectives and sparks to take with us on our journey.

ii.

Body

Through physical practices we challenge our own belief system of what our bodies are capable of — priming ourselves to more fully receive and integrate everything else the weekend holds.

iii.

Human Spirit

Through giving and receiving without expectation we understand all is connected. The human spirit is the Jelly that forms a lasting, meaningful connection between all of us.

“This spirit manifests itself in everything we see, feel, touch, taste and hear at Breathe.”
