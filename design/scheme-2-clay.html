Who we are

Something greater
than ourselves.

BREATHE is a community grounded in the belief that everyone has a higher purpose. Alongside Mother Nature we manifest an environment for each individual to discover that purpose, write their own story, and achieve their highest potential.

The workshops, classes, performances, discussions, walks, and games that fill the weekend aren't created by a hired programming team. They're created by people in this community who are passionate about something and feel called to share it. Over the years that has meant medicinal plant walks, movement practices, storytelling circles, slackline workshops, music experiences, philosophy discussions, creative arts, breathwork, and countless things we never could have imagined on our own.

We dissolve the barriers between the seen and the unseen, revealing a community that is whole, inter-connected, and free.

We gather at Deerfields — a beautiful natural setting that invites us to slow down, connect, and live simply together for the weekend. Primitive camping, well water, outdoor showers, and a shared meal tent. No vendors. No spectacle. Just a community building the thing it wants to attend.

What holds us together

We are anchored by the balancing of mind, body, and human spirit.

i.

Mind

Through the selfless service of sharing our passions as teachers we build confidence, initiative, and connection. As students we gain new perspectives, inspiration, and sparks to take with us on our journey.

ii.

Body

Through physical practices we challenge our own belief system of what our bodies are capable of — priming us to more fully receive, absorb, and integrate the intellectual and spiritual spectrums of Breathe.

iii.

Human Spirit

Through giving and receiving without expectation we understand all is connected. The human spirit is the Jelly that forms a lasting, meaningful connection between all of us here at Breathe.

Plan your visit

Deerfields.
Horse Shoe, NC.

Twenty minutes south of Asheville, and twenty from the airport. Primitive camping is included with a weekend pass, in Elysian Fields and Valhalla. Free overnight parking is onsite, within walking distance of both campgrounds.

Potable well water is free; ice is available to purchase. The facility has no indoor toilets or showers — we rely on port-o-johns and outdoor showers. Come prepared to be self-reliant, and don't hesitate to lean on a neighbor to fill the gaps.

There are no food vendors onsite. A Community Meal Prep Tent has shared stoves, tables, chairs, and basic utensils. Bring your own propane and specialty supplies.
