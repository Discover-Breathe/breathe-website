What changed from the last round

  • The top bar is opaque in all three. It was translucent over the hero photo, which is why it vanished. Contrast is now guaranteed against a flat colour (6.3–8.9:1) rather than against whatever the photograph happens to be.
  • #a8501c is gone. That was a colour I invented, not one from your site. Every accent here is a real step from your own ramps.
  • Contrast re-verified on every text node at 390 px and 1440 px: zero WCAG AA failures in all three.
  • One trade-off worth knowing: the mid-tone accents (#4A818E, #5B899B) are too light to carry a button label at 4.5:1, so the primary CTA uses the next deeper step. That's why the buttons look slightly darker than the decorative accent.
