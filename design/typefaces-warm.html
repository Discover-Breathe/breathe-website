BREATHE · Warmer typefaces

Four earthier options

Same Stacked layout, same teal. These four move toward humanist and organic rather than geometric — the letterforms come from handwriting and calligraphy rather than compass and ruler. That's the technical difference behind "warmer".

"Industrious hippies." That's a precise brief, and it rules out two easy mistakes:
  • Not tie-dye whimsy — rounded novelty faces would read as costume, not craft.
  • Not corporate neutrals — the geometric grotesques we just looked at are competent but bloodless.
  • Capable and unpretentious — people who build slackline parks, cook for 300, and rig a stage, but who also talk about the Jelly without irony.
So: humanist skeletons, slightly open apertures, a little irregularity in the curves, and enough weight range to carry a big headline without looking flimsy.
