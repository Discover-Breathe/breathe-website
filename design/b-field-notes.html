Our mission
Community ✳

Belonging, by default

We foster an environment filled with belonging, compassion, and love — dissolving the barriers between the seen and unseen to reveal a community that is whole, inter-connected, and free.

The Jelly ✳

Mind, body, spirit

Three things that only make sense together. Movement primes the body. Teaching sharpens the mind. Giving without expectation is what actually connects us.

The weekend ✳

Live simply, together

Primitive camping, well water, outdoor showers, a shared meal-prep tent, and no vendors. Self-sufficient by design, so the weekend belongs to the people in it.

The experience

Six ways in
1.1

Slacklining & highlines

Parks, waterlines, and long highlines strung through the trees — from first steps to the 1km record line.

↗ 1.2

Yoga & movement

Daily practices on the land, from restorative flow to strength work. All levels, all bodies.

↗ 1.3

Drumming & music

DRUMeditation, community rhythm circles, and live sets from musicians in the community.

↗ 1.4

Workshops & talks

Plant walks, philosophy, breathwork, creative arts, skills, and the Condor Talk Tent.

↗ 1.5

Kids & families

Children's activities through the weekend. Kids 12 and under come free.

↗ 1.6

The land itself

Ponds, forests, and trails at Deerfields. Leave no trace, and leave it better.

Plan your visit

Deerfields.
Horse Shoe, NC.

Twenty minutes south of Asheville, and twenty from the airport. Primitive camping in Elysian Fields and Valhalla, free potable well water, outdoor showers, and ice for sale on site.

There are no food vendors. Bring and prepare your own meals — or cook alongside everyone else at the Community Meal Prep Tent, with shared stoves, tables, and basic utensils.
